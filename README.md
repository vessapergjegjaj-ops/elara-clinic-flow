# Aura Aesthetics

Create a premium, modern, and high-end aesthetic dermatology and beauty clinic web application.

BRAND CONCEPT & DESIGN DIRECTION:

- Visual Style: Editorial, luxurious, warm, clean, and feminine.

- Color Palette: Warm Alabaster/Beige (#FDFBF7), Champagne Gold accents (#D4AF37 / #C5A880), Muted Taupe/Soft Rose, Pure White, and Deep Charcoal/Off-Black for high-contrast typography.

- Typography: Elegant Serif for primary headings (e.g., Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) combined with a crisp, modern Sans-Serif for body text (e.g., Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter).

- UI Details: Smooth scroll, soft micro-interactions, subtle glassmorphism overlay cards, rounded corners (12px to 24px), refined layout spacing, and elegant hover effects with soft image zooms (1.05x).

PAGES & SECTIONS TO INCLUDE:

1. NAVIGATION & HEADER:

   - Floating/Sticky blur backdrop header.

   - Logo placeholder: "AESTHETICA CLINIC" or "ORHIDEA AESTHETICS".

   - Links: Home, Treatments, Before & After, Team, Pricing, About, Contact.

   - Action Buttons: "Book Appointment" (primary CTA) and "Admin Login" (icon/button).

2. HOME PAGE HERO SECTION:

   - Editorial video/image background showcasing a luxury clinic atmosphere and radiant glowing skin.

   - Headline: "The Art of Natural Beauty & Advanced Skin Science."

   - Subheadline: "Leading aesthetic dermatology clinic offering bespoke facial transformations, fillers, and laser treatments."

   - Buttons: "Book Consultation" and "Explore Treatments".

   - Quick stats banner: 10+ Years Experience, 15,000+ Satisfied Clients, Board-Certified Doctors.

3. BEFORE & AFTER INTERACTIVE GALLERY:

   - Interactive image slider comparison (drag handle left/right to reveal before & after).

   - Category filter tabs: Lips & Fillers, Anti-Wrinkle Botox, Non-Surgical Rhinoplasty, Hydrafacial & Skin Quality.

   - Responsive, touch-friendly slider for mobile users.

4. TREATMENTS SHOWCASE (SERVICE CARDS):

   - Categorized grid: Dermal Fillers, Botox, Laser Hair Removal, Skin Boosters, Facials & Peels.

   - Each card contains: Treatment Title, Duration (e.g., 30 mins), Results Duration (e.g., 6-12 months), short description, price range, and a "Quick View" / "Book This" modal trigger.

5. ONLINE BOOKING SYSTEM MODAL:

   - 3-step interactive booking flow:

     Step 1: Select Treatment category & specific procedure.

     Step 2: Choose Doctor/Specialist and select Date & Available Time slot from an interactive calendar.

     Step 3: Enter Client details (Name, Phone number, Email, Notes) + "Confirm Booking" button with immediate success animation.

6. DOCTORS & CERTIFICATIONS SECTION:

   - Clean profile cards for dermatologists and aesthetic specialists with photos, titles, qualifications, and badges (e.g., "Allergan Certified Injector", "FDA Cleared Equipment").

7. REVIEWS & TESTIMONIALS:

   - Premium card slider featuring client reviews, star ratings, and treatment tags.

8. WHATSAPP & CONTACT SECTION:

   - Sticky floating WhatsApp quick-chat widget on bottom right.

   - Contact info block: Address in Peja, Kosovo, Working Hours, Phone, Direct WhatsApp link, and embedded Google Map placeholder.

9. ADMIN DASHBOARD & AUTHENTICATION:

   - Secure Admin Login Page (/admin/login) with Email/Password inputs.

   - Full Admin Dashboard Interface (/admin/dashboard) featuring:

     * Overview Cards: Total Bookings, Revenue, Pending Appointments, New Messages.

     * Appointments Table: Filter by date/status, approve/cancel appointments, view client details.

     * Image Upload Manager: Simple drag-and-drop or mobile photo picker interface for "Before & After" gallery and new treatment photos (supports file upload preview regardless of extension).

     * Treatments Manager: Add/Edit/Delete treatments, prices, and descriptions.

     * Messages & Inquiries inbox.

MOBILE EXPERIENCE:

- Highly responsive touch UX, sticky bottom action bar ("Book Now" + "WhatsApp") on mobile viewports.
